Safety & Safeguarding

Student welfare comes first

Safety is the foundation every expedition is built on. From the camps we own to the teams who lead each trip, every part of a Camps expedition is designed to protect the students in your care. The detail below is covered fully in our Teacher Handbook, which we will walk through with you.

Accredited and assessed
Camps International holds the Council for Learning Outside the Classroom Quality Badge and complies with BS8848, the British Standard for overseas expeditions. We are a member of the Expedition Providers Association and our standards are independently re-assessed.
Trusted operations and providers
We leverage our own permanent operations alongside vetted third-party providers, each of which undergoes rigorous due diligence before any student travels. Accommodation, security and standards are checked against the same Camps International requirements.
ADEK and KHDA aligned ratios
Staff-to-student ratios meet ADEK and KHDA requirements, with accompanying teachers and an experienced, qualified Camps Expedition Leader on every expedition.
Safeguarding and DBS checks
Every member of expedition staff is background-checked, including DBS clearance. We hold a comprehensive child protection policy with named safeguarding leads in-country and at head office, sitting alongside your school’s own policy.
Robust risk management
Every expedition carries a full risk assessment, reviewed and updated ahead of each departure. We provide the risk assessments and crisis procedures your school needs to make the sign-off process straightforward.
Cover and crisis response
Every expedition is fully insured, with a 24-hour on-call service across every region, established medical facilities and clear casualty and evacuation procedures behind every trip.
A school expedition, with the protections that brings

This is a school expedition. It is organised and run as a school trip and benefits from all the relevant protections that apply, including consumer financial protection and established safety governance.

Ahead of departure your regional coordinator will share an Expedition Agreement, specific to your school and your chosen expedition. It sets out clearly the role, obligations and responsibilities of Camps International to you, your pupils and your wider school community, so everyone is clear on exactly what we are accountable for.

The Camps difference

Why Choose Camps

Plenty of providers can book a flight and a hotel. Choosing an expedition partner is a different decision. It is about what your students actually do when they arrive, who looks after them while they are there, and what they carry home. This is where Camps International is built differently, and we would encourage you to compare closely.

Permanent, owned infrastructure
We own and run our own network of permanent camps, staffed year round by our own in-country teams. We are not assembling a one-off trip from third-party brokers for a single departure. That permanence is what makes genuine long-term community and conservation work possible, and what lets us keep your students safe in places we know intimately.
Real, long-term impact
Our projects are not set pieces arranged for visiting groups. They are stages in long-term programmes that were running before your students arrive and continue long after they fly home, developed with the communities themselves. Students contribute to something real and measurable.
Curriculum that earns its place
Every expedition is mapped against your curriculum, whether the British, American or IB curricula followed across the region, so the trip justifies itself academically as well as pastorally. The learning links are specific and evidenced, not a generic claim about broadening horizons.
The ten Transformational Outcomes
We are explicit about the growth a Camps expedition develops, framed around ten Transformational Outcomes, from resilience and self-reliance to cross-cultural intelligence. That gives you and your parents an honest account of what students come home with, and gives students strong personal-statement evidence for university.
What our model allows for
✓
Transport we arrange and vet
Our model allows for all transport to be either our own vehicles or vetted, approved third-party providers, every one checked against our own standards. Students are never left to use public transport or navigate connections themselves.
✓
Everything organised for you
Our model allows for every part of the itinerary to be arranged and paid for in advance through our own infrastructure. There is nothing for students to budget for or settle with providers while on expedition, it is all taken care of for them.
✓
Food and allergy management
Our model allows for meals to be prepared in our own camps or by approved providers working to our standards, so allergies and dietary requirements are managed as a matter of course rather than left to roadside stops.
